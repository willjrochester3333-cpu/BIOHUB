Everything needed to build the regular Minnow design, grouped by the shop you would order it from. Generated by `bom/make_shopping_list.py` from the same data as [`bom.csv`](bom.csv), so quantities and prices match the parts list.

**About the links.** They are *search* links for the exact part, not links to one listing: listings on AliExpress, eBay and Amazon change weekly, and a search keeps working. Pick a listing that matches the "Check" column. Prices are rough UK prices (late 2026), delivered; check them before you buy.

## Order 2: JLCPCB (the main board) - about £14

1. Go to the [JLCPCB quote page](https://cart.jlcpcb.com/quote) and upload [`pcb/output/auv-mainboard-gerbers.zip`](../pcb/output/auv-mainboard-gerbers.zip).
2. It reads the size by itself (150 x 60 mm, 2 layers). Leave thickness 1.6 mm, quantity 5, HASL lead-free. Any colour.
3. Do not tick PCB assembly: every part is 0805 or bigger, so it is easy to solder by hand.
4. Alternative: [PCBWay](https://www.pcbway.com/orderonline.aspx) takes the same zip file.

The fuse holder must have two 2 mm pins 9.2 mm apart plus a locating peg (the Littelfuse / Pudenz 2-pin ATO holder). Check the drawing in the datasheet before ordering. Add one **15 A ATO blade fuse** (any car shop, or [Amazon UK](https://www.amazon.co.uk/s?k=15A+ATO+blade+fuse)).

## Not on this list

## Before you click buy

1. Order AliExpress and the PCB first: they take 1-3 weeks. Print the O-ring fit test while you wait.
2. Measure the pipe as soon as it arrives (outside diameter in several places). If it is not 90.0 mm, change `pipe_od` in `cad/build_sub.py` before printing the end caps.
3. Check the camera and the battery pack sizes against the CAD (`cam_*`, `batt_*` in `build_sub.py`) before printing the sled and cradle.
