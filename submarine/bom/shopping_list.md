# Minnow shopping list (no soldering)

Everything needed to build Minnow **without soldering anything**, grouped by the shop you would order it from. The electronics are the [Minnow Core board](../pcb-core/README.md), which JLCPCB assembles completely; every other connection is a plug, a screw terminal, a lever connector or a crimp splice, and the 3D-printed parts need no heat-set inserts (screws cut their own thread, and the end-cap retainers use glued studs and nuts), so **nothing in the build needs a soldering iron or any other hot tool except a heat gun or lighter for the splices**. Generated by `bom/make_shopping_list.py` (the rev 1 build, which you solder, is in [`shopping_list_rev1.md`](shopping_list_rev1.md)).

**About the links.** They are *search* links for the exact part, not links to one listing: listings on Amazon and eBay change weekly, and a search keeps working. Pick a listing that matches the "Check" column. Prices are rough UK prices (late 2026), delivered; check them before you buy.

## How everything connects without solder

| Connection | How |
|---|---|
| Battery to board | XT60 plug (already on the battery) |
| Board to ESCs (power) | XT30 lead from the board, joined to the four ESC power leads with two Wago lever connectors |
| ESC signal wires, GPS, depth sensor, leak probes, strobe, AUX | Screw terminals on the board: strip 5 mm, push in, tighten |
| ESC motor wires to the thruster cables, cables to the motors | Heat-shrink crimp splices (crimp, then heat; the glue seals the ones in the water) |
| Depth sensor | Pins already fitted, female-to-male jumper wires into the screw terminals |
| Camera, antennas, laptop | Plugs: USB-A, U.FL, USB-C |

## Not on this list

* Leak probes: nothing to buy. Use two bare wire ends (an offcut of the thruster cable) per probe.
* The relay buoy and underwater sound link discussed earlier are not designed yet.
* Printed parts: they come out of the 1 kg of PETG above. See the print list in [`docs/01-mechanical.md`](../docs/01-mechanical.md#111-print-list).

## Before you click buy

1. Order the boards first: they take 1-2 weeks. Print the O-ring fit test while you wait.
2. Measure the pipe as soon as it arrives (outside diameter in several places). If it is not 90.0 mm, change `pipe_od` in `cad/build_sub.py` before printing the end caps.
3. Check the camera and the battery pack sizes against the CAD (`cam_*`, `batt_*` in `build_sub.py`) before printing the sled and cradle.
